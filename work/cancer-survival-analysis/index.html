Cancer patient survival analysis

How long do patients live after a cancer diagnosis, and how much of that can routine clinical data explain? This team project follows 988 patients, compares survival across tumor stages, and tests whether machine learning can predict each patient's survival time.

Data

The dataset has 988 patients and 13 clinical fields: age at diagnosis, vital status, tumor stage and grade, residual disease, primary therapy outcome, cancer status, overall and progression-free survival, and platinum-free interval and status. Patient IDs follow The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) barcode format.

Approach

  1. Cleaned the data: median imputation for numbers, mode for categories, encoding, standardization, and an 80/20 train and test split.
  2. Explored distributions, category breakdowns and correlations.
  3. Estimated Kaplan-Meier survival curves by patient group with lifelines.
  4. Trained Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, SVR and XGBoost regressors on overall survival, scored by MAE, RMSE and R-squared.
